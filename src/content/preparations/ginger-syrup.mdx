<Step id="grate">
  Grate the <Qty ref="ginger"/> coarsely, skin and all. Peeling costs flavour and buys nothing here, because none of it stays in the bottle.
</Step>

<Step id="dissolve">
  Warm the <Qty ref="water"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> until clear, then stir the grated root through and take the pan off the heat.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Leave to steep as it cools, then strain through a fine sieve, pressing the pulp to get the syrup back out of it.
</Step>
