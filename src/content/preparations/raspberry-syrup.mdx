<Step id="cook">
  Warm the <Qty ref="raspberries"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a saucepan, crushing the fruit
  as it softens, until the sugar has fully dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Pass the mixture through a fine sieve, pressing the solids to extract the syrup and discarding
  the seeds left behind.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool and bottle.
</Step>
