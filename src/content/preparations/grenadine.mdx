<Step id="dissolve">
  Warm the <Qty ref="juice"/> gently and stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> until it has gone. Keep it below a simmer; reducing it makes a thicker syrup than the recipe describes and every figure on this page then understates it.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool and bottle. It keeps less well than a plain syrup because the juice in it is food as well as sugar.
</Step>
