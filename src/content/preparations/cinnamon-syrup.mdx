<Step id="break">
  Break the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> into pieces. Broken bark gives up far more than whole sticks do, which is the difference between a syrup that tastes of cinnamon and one that smells faintly of it.
</Step>

<Step id="dissolve">
  Warm the <Qty ref="water"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> until clear, add the bark and take the pan off the heat.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Leave to steep as it cools, then strain. Steeping longer keeps deepening it, so taste it before straining rather than working to the clock.
</Step>
