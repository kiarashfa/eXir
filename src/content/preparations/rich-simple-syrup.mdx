<Step id="dissolve">
  Warm the <Qty ref="water"/> and stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> in two or three additions, waiting for each to go clear before the next. This is close to as much sugar as water will hold, so it wants heat and patience rather than more stirring.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool completely and bottle. If crystals form on the shoulder of the bottle in the fridge, warming it gently takes them back into solution.
</Step>
