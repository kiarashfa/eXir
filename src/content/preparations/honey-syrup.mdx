<Step id="warm">
  Warm the <Qty ref="water"/> to no more than <Temp c={50}/> and whisk in the <Qty ref="honey"/> until it is evenly mixed. Honey is worth keeping below a simmer: heat drives off the volatile aromatics that were the reason for reaching for honey rather than sugar.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool and bottle. It separates slightly on standing and needs a shake before use.
