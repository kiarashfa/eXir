<Step id="prep">
  Peel the zest from the <Qty ref="lime-zest"/>, avoiding the white pith, and slice the
  <Qty ref="ginger"/>. Lightly crush the <Qty ref="almonds"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Combine the zest, ginger, almonds and the <Qty ref="cloves"/> with the <Qty ref="rum"/> in a
  sealed jar and leave to steep at room temperature for a full day, shaking it whenever it is
  passed.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the solids through a fine sieve, pressing lightly to recover the rum.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Stir the infused rum into the <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and bottle.
</Step>
