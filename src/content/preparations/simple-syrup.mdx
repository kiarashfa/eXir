<Step id="dissolve">
  Warm the <Qty ref="water"/> until it steams, take it off the heat and stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> until the liquid runs completely clear. It does not need to boil, and boiling it only drives off water and concentrates the syrup past what the recipe says it is.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Cool to room temperature before bottling. Warm syrup going into a cold drink melts ice that the dilution model has already accounted for.
</Step>
