<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="almonds"/> to a coarse meal. Fine enough to give up their oil, coarse enough to strain.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Cover the meal with the <Qty ref="water"/>, warmed to about <Temp c={60}/>, and leave it to steep until cool.
</Step>

<Step id="press">
  Strain through muslin and wring the meal out hard. The last of what comes through is the richest part of it.
</Step>

<Step id="sweeten">
  Stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the almond milk until it is completely dissolved, then stir the <Qty ref="flower-water"/> through at the end, off the heat, where its aroma survives.
</Step>
