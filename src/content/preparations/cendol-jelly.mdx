<Step id="extract">
  Blend the <Qty ref="pandan"/> with the <Qty ref="pandan-water"/> and
  strain out the fibre, keeping the pale green liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="rice-flour"/> and the <Qty ref="tapioca"/> into the
  <Qty ref="cook-water"/> and the pandan liquid, then cook over medium
  heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens into a smooth, glossy,
  translucent paste.
</Step>

<Step id="set">
  Push the hot paste through a colander or a cendol mould directly into a
  bowl of iced water, letting it fall in short strands that firm up on
  contact with the cold water.
</Step>
