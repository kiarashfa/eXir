<Step id="simmer">
  Simmer the <Qty ref="sassafras"/>, the <Qty ref="sarsaparilla"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/>
  together, then stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> until it dissolves. Add the
  <Qty ref="wintergreen"/> in the last few minutes of the simmer and take the pan off the heat.

<Step id="steep">

</Step>

<Step id="strain">

</Step>
