<Step id="simmer">
  Add the sliced <Qty ref="ginger"/> to the <Qty ref="water"/> in a small pan,
  bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for <Dur step="simmer"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a mug and discard the spent root.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Let the liquid cool for a moment off the heat, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="honey"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon"/>.
</Step>
