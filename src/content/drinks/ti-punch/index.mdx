<Step id="twist">
  Cut a small piece from the <Qty ref="lime"/> and twist it over the glass
  to release its oil, then drop it in.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="rhum"/>, and stir briefly with
  a swizzle stick or spoon. No ice goes in the glass.
</Step>
