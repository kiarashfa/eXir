<Step id="warm">
  Combine the <Qty ref="wine"/>, the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="cloves"/>, the <Qty ref="nutmeg"/>,
  the <Qty ref="orange"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon"/> in a pot and warm
  gently until the sugar dissolves and the wine steams.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Take off the heat, cover and let the spices steep for <Dur step="steep"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Warm through again briefly and strain hot into small cups, floating a
  clove-studded slice of the <Qty ref="garnish-orange"/> on top of each.
</Step>
