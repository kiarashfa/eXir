<Step id="mix">
  Stir the <Qty ref="grapefruit"/> and the <Qty ref="cinnamon-syrup"/> together to make Don's Mix.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="jamaican-rum"/>, the <Qty ref="puerto-rican-rum"/>, the
  <Qty ref="overproof-rum"/>, Don's Mix, the <Qty ref="falernum"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the
  <Qty ref="grenadine"/>, the <Qty ref="absinthe"/> and the <Qty ref="bitters"/> in a blender
  jug, then add the <Qty ref="ice"/>, cracked.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Pulse for no more than <Dur step="blend"/> — long enough to chill and aerate it, not to turn

</Step>
