<Step id="knead">
  Knead the <Qty ref="kava"/> by hand inside a cloth bag submerged in the
  <Qty ref="water"/>, working it for <Dur step="knead"/> until the water
  has turned cloudy and muddy brown.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Wring the bag out hard to get the last of the extract through, then
  ladle into shells or cups, stirring or swirling each one just before it
  is handed over.
</Step>
