<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> to chill and aerate the drink before any
  soda reaches it.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain up into a chilled highball glass, with no ice in the glass itself.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/>, poured gently down the side of the glass
  so the foam it raises has somewhere to go, and serve at once.
</Step>
