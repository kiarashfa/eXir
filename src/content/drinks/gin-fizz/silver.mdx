<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="syrup"/>
  and the <Qty ref="egg"/> in a shaker, with no ice yet.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="dry-shake"/> with no ice, so the egg white has
  room to whip into a stable foam before any water is added to it.
</Step>

<Step id="wet-shake">
  Add cubed ice and shake again for <Dur step="wet-shake"/> to chill the
  drink and finish the emulsion.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Fine strain up into a chilled highball glass, with no ice in the glass
  itself.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/>, poured gently down the side of the glass
  so the foam already sitting on the drink is not broken, and serve at once.
</Step>
