<Step id="build">
  Build the <Qty ref="vodka"/>, the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="rum"/>,
  the <Qty ref="tequila"/>, the <Qty ref="curacao"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>
  and the <Qty ref="syrup"/> over cubed ice in a collins glass.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="cola"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir briefly, just enough to bring the cola through the rest without
  beating out its fizz.
</Step>
