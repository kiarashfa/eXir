  - kind: technique
    stepRef: build
    text: >-
      The syrup here runs above what the shaken Daiquiri takes, because a
      drink served this cold reads markedly less sweet than the same liquid
      would at room temperature. The correction has to be made before the
      ice goes in, since there is no tasting a slush and adjusting it after.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Blending in short bursts keeps the drink cold; a single long run heats
      the jug against the blade and comes back looser and warmer than the
      same ice would if pulsed. It should look like wet sand climbing the
      sides, not a liquid with chips floating in it.

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the <Qty ref="rum"/> and the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a blender jug, then add the <Qty ref="ice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend in short bursts for <Dur step="blend"/>, until the mixture climbs the
  sides of the jug as an even, pourable slush with no loose chunks left
  clicking against the blade.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour, unstrained, into a chilled poco grande glass and serve with a straw
  before it starts to separate.
</Step>
