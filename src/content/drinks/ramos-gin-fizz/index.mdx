<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the
  pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>,
  the <Qty ref="syrup"/>, the <Qty ref="flower-water"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cream"/> and the <Qty ref="egg"/> in a shaker, with no ice yet.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="dry-shake"/> with no ice. This is the step the
  drink is named for: the cream and egg white need that much time and force
  to build the dense foam that holds its shape in the glass.
</Step>

<Step id="wet-shake">
  Add cubed ice and shake again for <Dur step="wet-shake"/> to chill the
  drink and finish the emulsion.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Fine strain up into a chilled Collins glass, with no ice in the glass
  itself.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/>, poured gently down the side of the glass so
  the foam rises above the rim rather than overflowing it, and serve at once.
</Step>
