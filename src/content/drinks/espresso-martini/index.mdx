<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> fine for the espresso machine.
</Step>

<Step id="extract">
  Pump the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={93}/> for
  <Dur step="extract"/> and let the shot cool for a minute.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="vodka"/>, <Qty ref="liqueur"/>, hot shot and
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a shaker with ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>, until the tin frosts and the foam
  builds thick against the strainer.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled glass and set the <Qty ref="garnish"/> on the
  crema.
</Step>
