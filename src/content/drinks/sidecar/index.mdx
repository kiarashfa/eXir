<Step id="rim">
  Run a lemon wedge around half the rim of a coupe and dip that half in
  sugar.
</Step>

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="cognac"/>, the <Qty ref="liqueur"/> and the
  <Qty ref="lemon"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> until the tin frosts over.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into the rimmed glass, which holds no ice of its own.
</Step>
