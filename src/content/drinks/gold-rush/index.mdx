notes:
  - kind: technique
    stepRef: strain
    text: >-
      Strained over fresh ice rather than up, unlike most of the family it
      belongs to. Bourbon's own weight carries the honey's roundness better
      cold and slightly diluted further than it does straight from the
      shaker, where the two together can read heavy.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Honey syrup, not honey straight from the jar. Raw honey is too viscous
      to combine evenly in a cold shake and sinks to the bottom of the glass
      rather than mixing through it; let down with water first, it pours and
      measures like any other sweetener in the family.

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="bourbon"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the
  <Qty ref="honey"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill
  and aerate, not to melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a double old fashioned glass over fresh cubed ice.
</Step>
