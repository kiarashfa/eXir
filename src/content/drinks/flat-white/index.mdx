<Step id="steam">
  Steam the <Qty ref="milk"/> to a glossy microfoam, pulling the wand out
  before a dry cap has time to build.
</Step>

<Step id="extract">
  Pump the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={93}/> for
  <Dur step="extract"/> while the milk steams.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">

  with a thin, level surface rather than a raised cap.
</Step>
