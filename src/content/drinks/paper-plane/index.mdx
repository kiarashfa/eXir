<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="bourbon"/>, the <Qty ref="nonino"/>, the
  <Qty ref="aperol"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon"/> in a shaker and fill it with
  cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Four equal measures chill and dilute
  exactly as fast as any other shaken sour.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled coupe, catching the ice shards the shake has
  broken off.
</Step>
