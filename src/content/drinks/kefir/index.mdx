<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="milk"/> and <Qty ref="grains"/> in a clean jar and
  stir gently to distribute them.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  away from direct light.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the grains, reserving them for the next batch, and refrigerate
  the kefir before serving.
