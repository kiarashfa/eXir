<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="campari"/>, the
  <Qty ref="pineapple"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/> and the <Qty ref="syrup"/> in
  a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> to chill and knit the juice's sugar
  against the Campari's bitterness.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain over fresh cubed ice in a double old fashioned glass. The pineapple
  leaves enough pulp behind in the shaker that a fine strain is not needed.
</Step>
