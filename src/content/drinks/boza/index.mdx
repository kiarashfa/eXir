<Step id="cook">
  Simmer the <Qty ref="millet"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/>, stirring
  occasionally, until it collapses into a soft, thick porridge.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Take it off the heat and let it cool fully to room temperature before
  anything else goes in, or the next step's yeast will not survive it.
</Step>

<Step id="inoculate">
  Stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="yeast"/> through the cooled
  porridge until both are evenly distributed.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover the jar loosely and leave it at room temperature for
  <Dur step="ferment"/>, away from direct light, until it smells mildly
  tart rather than sharply alcoholic.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the settled grain, pressing to get the last of the liquid
  through, and refrigerate until cold. Finish each glass with the
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, grated straight over the top.
</Step>
