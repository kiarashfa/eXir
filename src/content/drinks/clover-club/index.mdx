<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="raspberry"/> and the
  <Qty ref="egg"/> in a shaker, with no ice yet.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="dry-shake"/> with no ice, so the egg white has room to whip into a
  stable foam before any water is added to it.
</Step>

<Step id="wet-shake">
  Add cubed ice and shake again for <Dur step="wet-shake"/> to chill the drink and finish the
  emulsion.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Fine strain into a chilled coupe, so none of the ice shards from the second shake reach the
  glass to break the foam.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Set a raspberry or two on a pick laid across the rim, resting on the foam rather than sinking
  into it.
</Step>
