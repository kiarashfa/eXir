<Step id="fill">
  Fill the gourd about two-thirds full with the <Qty ref="mate"/>, cover
  the mouth and shake once, then tip the gourd so the leaf banks to one
  side. Set the bombilla into the empty side, against the wall of the
  gourd rather than straight down into the leaf.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Pour the <Qty ref="water"/>, just off the boil, slowly into the empty
  side and let it soak into the leaf for <Dur step="steep"/> before the
  first sip.
</Step>

<Step id="sip">
  Drink the full gourd through the bombilla without stirring, then refill
  with fresh hot water for the next round.
</Step>
