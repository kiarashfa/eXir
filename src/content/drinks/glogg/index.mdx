<Step id="steep">
  Warm the <Qty ref="wine"/> with the <Qty ref="sugar"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cardamom"/>, the <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>, the <Qty ref="cloves"/>
  and the <Qty ref="orange"/> zest over low heat, then take it off the heat
  and let it steep, covered, for <Dur step="steep"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="warm">
  Return to low heat and warm through for <Dur step="warm"/>, then stir in
  the <Qty ref="aquavit"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Divide the <Qty ref="raisins"/> and <Qty ref="almonds"/> between mugs and
  strain the hot wine over them.
</Step>
