<Step id="rinse">
  Chill a rocks glass. Pour the <Qty ref="rinse"/> into it, tilt and swirl so
  the spirit coats the inside completely, then tip it out over the sink.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="syrup"/>, the <Qty ref="bitters"/> and the
  <Qty ref="rye"/> in a mixing glass filled with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into the rinsed glass, which holds no ice of its own.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a <Len cm={4}/> swathe of lemon peel, hold it skin-outwards over the
  surface and squeeze so the oil lands on the drink, then discard the peel
  rather than dropping it in.
</Step>
