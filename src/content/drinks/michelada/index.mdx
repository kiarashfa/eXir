notes:
  - kind: technique
    stepRef: build
    text: >-

      seasoned rather than plain lager with seasoning still settling at the
      bottom.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      A wet, over-poured rim washes the <Qty ref="salt"/> straight into the
      glass and salts the whole drink at once; the rim is meant to be
      tasted a sip at a time, not stirred in.

<Step id="rim">
  Run a lime wedge around half the glass's rim and dip it in the
  <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="lime"/> and the <Qty ref="hotsauce"/> to the chilled
  glass.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top slowly with the <Qty ref="beer"/>, tilting the glass to keep the foam
  down.
</Step>
