<Step id="rim">
  Run a lime wedge around half the glass's rim and dip it in the
  <Qty ref="salt"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, the
  <Qty ref="worcestershire"/> and the <Qty ref="hotsauce"/> to the chilled
  glass.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top slowly with the <Qty ref="beer"/>, tilting the glass to keep the foam
  down.
</Step>
