notes:
  - kind: technique
    stepRef: sink
    text: >-
      Grenadine is denser than the orange juice above it, so it is poured
      down the inside of the glass and left alone rather than stirred; it
      settles to the bottom and rises through the juice in streaks on its
      own, which is the "sunrise" the name describes.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Stirring the drink before it is served mixes the layers into a flat
      orange and loses the effect entirely; it is meant to be stirred once,
      by the drinker, at the table.

<Step id="build">
  Build the <Qty ref="tequila"/> and the <Qty ref="orange"/> over cubed ice
  in a highball glass.
</Step>

<Step id="sink">
  Pour the <Qty ref="grenadine"/> slowly down the inside of the glass and
  let it settle to the bottom without stirring.
</Step>
