<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="pineapple"/>, the
  <Qty ref="coconut"/> and the <Qty ref="orange"/> in a shaker with cubed
  ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> until the coconut cream is fully
  worked through the juice.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour unstrained into a tiki mug packed with crushed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Grate the <Qty ref="garnish-nutmeg"/> over the top.
</Step>
