<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and the <Qty ref="grapefruit"/> and strain
  out the pips and pulp from both.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the
  <Qty ref="grapefruit"/> and the <Qty ref="maraschino"/> in a shaker and
  fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill
  and aerate, not to melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled coupe, catching the ice shards the shake has
  broken off.
</Step>
