<Step id="fill">
  Fill a Collins glass with ice.
</Step>

<Step id="squeeze">
  Squeeze a lime wedge over the ice for the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and drop
  the spent wedge in.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="rum"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="cola"/> and lift once with a bar spoon rather than
  stirring, so the bubbles survive the trip to the table.
</Step>
