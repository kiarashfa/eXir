<Step id="chill">
  Chill a rocks glass.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rye"/>, the <Qty ref="cognac"/>, the
  <Qty ref="vermouth"/>, the <Qty ref="benedictine"/>, the
  <Qty ref="peychauds"/> and the <Qty ref="angostura"/> in a mixing glass
  filled with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted, and
  strain into the chilled glass over fresh ice.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a <Len cm={4}/> swathe of lemon peel, hold it skin-outwards over the
  surface and squeeze so the oil lands on the drink. Rub it around the rim
  and drop it in.
</Step>
