<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/> and the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill
  and aerate, not to melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled coupe, catching the ice shards the shake has
  broken off.
</Step>
