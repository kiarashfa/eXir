<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pisco"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="egg"/> in a shaker, with no ice yet.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="dry-shake"/> with no ice, so the egg white has
  room to whip into a stable foam before any water is added to it.
</Step>

<Step id="wet-shake">
  Add cubed ice and shake again for <Dur step="wet-shake"/> to chill the
  drink and finish the emulsion.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Fine strain into a chilled coupe, so none of the ice shards from the second
  shake reach the glass to break the foam.
</Step>

<Step id="bitters">
  Set the <Qty ref="bitters"/> on the foam in a few drops, drawn through with
  a pick if a pattern is wanted; either way it sits on the surface rather
  than sinking in.
</Step>
