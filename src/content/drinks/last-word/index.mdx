<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="chartreuse"/>, the
  <Qty ref="maraschino"/> and the <Qty ref="lime"/> in a shaker and fill it
  with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Four equal measures chill and dilute
  exactly as fast as any other shaken sour; nothing about equal parts asks
  for anything different here.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled coupe, catching the ice shards the shake has
  broken off.
</Step>
