<Step id="toast">
  Toast the <Qty ref="bread"/> in a low oven until it is dark and dry
  almost to the point of burning.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Pour the boiling <Qty ref="water"/> over the toasted bread and leave it to
  steep as it cools to room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="inoculate">
  Strain out the bread, then stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the
  <Qty ref="yeast"/> into the dark, toasty liquid until dissolved, and drop
  in the <Qty ref="raisins"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  away from direct light, until the raisins have risen to the top and the
  liquid tastes tangy and faintly fizzy.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into bottles, cap, and refrigerate until cold before serving.
</Step>
