<Step id="steam">
  Steam the <Qty ref="rice"/> until fully tender.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Spread the rice out and let it cool fully to room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Crumble the <Qty ref="nuruk"/> over the cooled rice and mix in the
  <Qty ref="mash-water"/>, working it through evenly.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover the crock loosely and leave it at room temperature for
  <Dur step="ferment"/>, stirring once a day for the first few days, until
  it smells sweet and yeasty and rises with visible bubbles.
</Step>

<Step id="press">
  Mash the ferment against a coarse cloth to press out the liquid, then
  stir in the <Qty ref="dilution-water"/> to loosen it to drinking
  strength, and refrigerate until cold. Shake the bottle before pouring to
  bring the settled rice back into suspension.
</Step>
