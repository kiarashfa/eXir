<Step id="rim">
  Run a lime wedge around half the rim of a coupe and dip that half in salt.
</Step>

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="tequila"/>, the <Qty ref="liqueur"/> and the
  <Qty ref="lime"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> until the tin frosts over.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into the rimmed glass, then set the lime wheel on the rim.
</Step>
