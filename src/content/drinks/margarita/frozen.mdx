<Step id="rim">
  Run a lime wedge around half the rim of a poco grande glass and dip that
  half in salt.
</Step>

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Combine the <Qty ref="tequila"/>, the <Qty ref="liqueur"/>, the
  <Qty ref="lime"/> and the <Qty ref="ice"/> in a blender and blend for
  <Dur step="blend"/> until it forms a uniform slush with no loose chunks.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour into the rimmed glass and set the lime wheel on the rim.
</Step>
