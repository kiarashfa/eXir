notes:
  - kind: technique
    text: >-
      Each layer goes in on its own rather than stirred together, so the syrup, noodles and
      seed sit visibly in the glass under the milk before the drink is mixed at the table — the

  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-

      before the glass reaches the table. It wants a slow pour down the side of the glass, the
      same as building any layered drink.

<Step id="layer">
  Spoon the <Qty ref="syrup"/> into the bottom of a tall glass, then add the
  <Qty ref="vermicelli"/> and the <Qty ref="basil-seeds"/> on top of it.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the cold <Qty ref="milk"/> slowly down the side of the glass to fill it.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Finish with the <Qty ref="ice-cream"/> on top and serve with a long spoon.
</Step>
