<Step id="chill">
  Chill the highball glass.
</Step>

<Step id="fill">
  Pack the chilled glass to the rim with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour in the <Qty ref="whisky"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir once, lifting the ice rather than crushing it, to bring the whisky
  up through the cubes.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/>, poured gently down a bar spoon so it
  keeps its fizz, and stir once more, briefly.
</Step>
