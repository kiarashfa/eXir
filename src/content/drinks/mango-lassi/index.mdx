<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="yoghurt"/>, the peeled <Qty ref="mango"/> and the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a blender jug, then add the <Qty ref="ice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend for <Dur step="blend"/>, until the mango is fully broken down and the
  ice is worked through into an even, pourable thickness.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour into a chilled lassi glass.
</Step>
