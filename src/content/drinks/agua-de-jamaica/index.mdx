<Step id="steep">
  Pour the <Qty ref="water"/> at <Temp c={100}/> over the <Qty ref="hibiscus"/>
  and the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and leave to steep for <Dur step="steep"/>. The
  liquid darkens to a deep red almost at once.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the calyces and discard them.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour over cubed ice in the glass and serve at once, while it is still cold
  from the ice rather than from having been chilled beforehand.
</Step>
