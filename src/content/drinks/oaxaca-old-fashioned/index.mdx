<Step id="chill">
  Chill a rocks glass and set a single large cube in it.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="tequila"/>, the <Qty ref="mezcal"/>, the
  <Qty ref="agave"/> and the <Qty ref="bitters"/> in a mixing glass filled
  with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted, and
  strain over the waiting cube.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a <Len cm={4}/> swathe of orange peel, warm it briefly skin-side down
  over a flame and hold it over the drink so the lit oil flares as it lands on
  the surface. Drop the peel in.
</Step>
