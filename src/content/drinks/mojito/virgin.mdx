<Step id="muddle">
  Muddle the <Qty ref="mint"/> gently with the <Qty ref="lime-juice"/> and
  the <Qty ref="syrup"/> in the base of a Collins glass.
</Step>

<Step id="fill">
  Fill the glass with ice.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/> and lift once with a bar spoon to bring the
  mint and lime up through the ice.
</Step>
