<Step id="open">
  Cut or punch an opening into the top of a chilled young coconut with a
  cleaver or a coconut opener.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the <Qty ref="coconut-water"/> into a glass, or serve directly from the
  shell with a straw.
</Step>
