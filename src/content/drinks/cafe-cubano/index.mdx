<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> fine and load it into the pot's basket.
</Step>

<Step id="brew">
  Brew the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={100}/> over
  <Dur step="brew"/>, catching the first few drops separately as they come
  through.
</Step>

<Step id="whip">
  Pour the reserved first drops over the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and whip
  vigorously for <Dur step="whip"/> until it lightens to a thick, pale tan
  foam.
</Step>

<Step id="fold">
  Pour in the rest of the brewed coffee and fold it through the foam without
  beating it flat, then serve at once.
</Step>
