<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="maraschino"/> and the
  <Qty ref="violette"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill and aerate, not to
  melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled coupe. Held to the light, the drink should read as a pale,
  smoke-tinted blue-grey rather than a true purple — the shade the name was coined for.
</Step>
