<Step id="fill">
  Fill a rocks glass with ice.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Pour the <Qty ref="campari"/> and the <Qty ref="vermouth"/> over the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/> and lift once with a bar spoon rather than
  stirring — the carbonation is doing work the stir would only knock out.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Drop in a half-wheel of orange.
</Step>
