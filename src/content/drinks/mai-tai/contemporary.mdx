<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Pack a double old fashioned glass with crushed ice, then pour in the
  <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the <Qty ref="curacao"/> and the
  <Qty ref="orgeat"/> straight over it.
</Step>

<Step id="swizzle">
  Plunge a swizzle stick to the bottom of the glass and spin it between the
  palms for <Dur step="swizzle"/>, working up and down through the ice until
  the outside of the glass frosts over.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Set the <Qty ref="mint"/> beside the straw so its aroma reaches the drinker
  on every sip.
</Step>
