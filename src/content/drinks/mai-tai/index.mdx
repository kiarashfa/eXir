<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the
  <Qty ref="curacao"/>, the <Qty ref="orgeat"/> and the <Qty ref="syrup"/> in
  a shaker with a scoop of crushed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="churn">
  Shake hard but briefly for <Dur step="churn"/>, just long enough to chill
  the drink without fully melting the ice it was shaken with.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the whole contents, ice and all, into a double old fashioned glass
  packed with fresh crushed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Set the <Qty ref="mint"/> beside the straw so its aroma reaches the drinker
  on every sip.
</Step>
