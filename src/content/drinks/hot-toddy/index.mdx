    text: >-
      The water is heated well short of a boil before it meets the
      <Qty ref="whisky"/>, since a full boil drives off some of the
      spirit's own alcohol and turns a hot drink meant to warm into one

  - kind: pitfall

<Step id="warm-glass">
  Warm the toddy mug with hot water, then empty it.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Stir the <Qty ref="whisky"/> and the <Qty ref="honey"/> together in the
  warmed mug until the honey loosens, then add the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cloves"/> and the hot <Qty ref="water"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Float the clove-studded <Qty ref="garnish-lemon"/> wheel on top.
</Step>
