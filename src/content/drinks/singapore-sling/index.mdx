<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="cherry"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cointreau"/>, the <Qty ref="benedictine"/>, the
  <Qty ref="pineapple"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the <Qty ref="grenadine"/>
  and the <Qty ref="bitters"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. The pineapple juice foams more than a
  citrus-only sour does, which is a sign the shake is working rather than a
  fault.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain up into a hurricane glass, with no ice in the glass itself. Between
  the juice and the shake's own dilution there is already too much liquid to
  add a fresh charge of ice without the glass running over.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a wedge from the <Qty ref="garnish-pineapple"/> and spear it on a pick
  with the <Qty ref="garnish-cherry"/>. Set it across the rim rather than
  dropping it in.
</Step>
