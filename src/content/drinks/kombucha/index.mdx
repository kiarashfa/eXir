<Step id="steep">
  Steep the <Qty ref="tea"/> in freshly boiled <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="steep"/>, then strain the leaves out.
</Step>

<Step id="dissolve">
  Stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the hot tea until it is completely
  dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the sweet tea to cool fully to room temperature before the next
  step.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Pour the cooled tea into a clean jar, lower in the <Qty ref="scoby"/>,
  cover with a breathable cloth secured with a band, and leave at room
  temperature for <Dur step="ferment"/>, away from direct light.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Lift out the SCOBY, reserving it for the next batch, and strain the
  kombucha into bottles for the refrigerator.
</Step>
