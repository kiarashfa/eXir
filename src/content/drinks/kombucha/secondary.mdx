<Step id="steep">
  Steep the <Qty ref="tea"/> in freshly boiled <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="steep"/>, then strain the leaves out.
</Step>

<Step id="dissolve">
  Stir the <Qty ref="primary-sugar"/> into the hot tea until it is
  completely dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="cool">
  Leave the sweet tea to cool fully to room temperature before the next
  step.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Pour the cooled tea into a clean jar, lower in the <Qty ref="scoby"/>,
  cover with a breathable cloth, and leave at room temperature for
  <Dur step="ferment"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="flavor">
  Lift out the SCOBY, then divide the kombucha among carbonation-rated
  bottles, dropping the <Qty ref="bottling-sugar"/> and the thinly sliced
  <Qty ref="ginger"/> into each before capping tightly.
</Step>

<Step id="condition">
  Leave the sealed bottles at room temperature for <Dur step="condition"/>,
  burping each once a day to release the building gas.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Strain out the ginger as you pour, over ice or straight into a chilled
  glass.
</Step>
