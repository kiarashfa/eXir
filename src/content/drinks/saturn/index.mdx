<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="passion-fruit"/>, the
  <Qty ref="orgeat"/> and the <Qty ref="falernum"/> in a blender with the <Qty ref="ice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend for <Dur step="blend"/>, just long enough to bring it to a smooth, pale slush.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour, unstrained, into a Collins glass.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Wind a long strip of lemon peel loosely around a cocktail cherry, pin it with a pick, and set
  it across the rim.
</Step>
