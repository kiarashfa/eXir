  Simmer the <Qty ref="plum"/>, the <Qty ref="hawthorn"/> and the
  <Qty ref="peel"/> in the <Qty ref="water"/> for <Dur step="boil"/>, until

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the solids and stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the hot
  liquid until fully dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="steep-osmanthus">
  Take the pot off the heat, stir in the <Qty ref="osmanthus"/>, and let
  it steep for <Dur step="steep-osmanthus"/> before straining it out too.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Refrigerate until fully cold before serving.
</Step>
