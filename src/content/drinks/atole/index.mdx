<Step id="slurry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="masa"/> into the <Qty ref="water"/> until completely
  smooth, with no dry lumps left.
</Step>

<Step id="simmer">
  Combine the masa slurry with the <Qty ref="milk"/>, <Qty ref="piloncillo"/>
  and <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> in a pot and simmer over <Dur step="simmer"/>,
  whisking constantly, until visibly thickened.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Remove the cinnamon stick and pour hot into the serving mug.
</Step>
