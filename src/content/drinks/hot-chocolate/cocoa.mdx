<Step id="paste">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="cocoa"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> with a small spoonful
  of the cold <Qty ref="milk"/> to a smooth paste.
</Step>

<Step id="heat">
  Warm the rest of the milk over <Dur step="heat"/>, without letting it
  boil, then whisk in the paste until fully dissolved.
</Step>
