<Step id="rinse">
  Pour a little of the <Qty ref="water"/> over the <Qty ref="tea"/> in the
  gaiwan and pour it straight off again, to wake the leaf without tasting it.
</Step>

<Step id="infuse">
  Pour the <Qty ref="water"/> at <Temp c={95}/> over the same leaf four
  times over, refilling the gaiwan fresh each round and lengthening the
  contact a little each time, for <Dur step="infuse"/> in total across the
  four rounds.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour each infusion off the leaf into the cup as soon as its time is up; a
  gaiwan left standing keeps extracting and the next round comes out uneven.
</Step>
