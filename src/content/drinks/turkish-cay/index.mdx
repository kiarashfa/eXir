<Step id="boil">
  Bring the <Qty ref="water"/> to a boil in the lower kettle.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Add the <Qty ref="tea"/> to the smaller pot set on top, ladle in a little
  of the boiling water, and steep for <Dur step="steep"/> over the
  simmering kettle below.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour a small amount of the concentrate into a tulip glass.
</Step>

<Step id="dilute">
  Top up with hot water from the lower kettle to the strength wanted.
</Step>
