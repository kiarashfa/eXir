<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="scotch"/>, the <Qty ref="cherry"/>, the
  <Qty ref="vermouth"/> and the <Qty ref="orange"/> in a shaker and fill it
  with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>, until the shaker is cold to the hand.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double-strain into a chilled coupe, leaving the orange juice's pulp and
  any ice chips behind.
</Step>
