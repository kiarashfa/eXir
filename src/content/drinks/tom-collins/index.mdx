<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake for <Dur step="shake"/> until the tin frosts over.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a Collins glass filled with fresh cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="soda"/> and drop in the cherry.
</Step>
