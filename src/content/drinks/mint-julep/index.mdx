<Step id="muddle">
  Press the mint for the <Qty ref="mint-muddle"/> gently in the bottom of a
  julep cup or rocks glass with the <Qty ref="syrup"/>, just enough to bruise
  the leaves.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="bourbon"/> and pack the cup with crushed ice, stirring as
  it goes in so the mint and syrup work their way through the ice rather than
  sitting at the bottom.
</Step>

<Step id="pack">
  Mound more crushed ice on top until it stands proud of the rim, and stir
  again for <Dur step="pack"/> from the surface down, until the outside of the
  cup is frosted white.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Stand the <Qty ref="mint-sprig"/> in the ice near a straw, so it sits right
  under the drinker's nose with every sip.
</Step>
