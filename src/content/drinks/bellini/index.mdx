<Step id="chill">
  Chill the flute. A Bellini has no ice in the glass, so the glass itself
  carries all of the cold.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Pour the <Qty ref="peach"/> into the chilled glass, then top with the
  <Qty ref="prosecco"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/> with a bar spoon to bring the puree up through
  the wine, rather than swirling the glass, which throws off the bead.
</Step>
