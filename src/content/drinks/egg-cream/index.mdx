<Step id="build">
  Pour the <Qty ref="syrup"/> into the bottom of a tall glass.
</Step>

<Step id="spray">
  Add the <Qty ref="milk"/>, then spray in the <Qty ref="soda"/> hard and
  fast, straight down the centre of the glass, for <Dur step="spray"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir just enough to lift the syrup up from the bottom, leaving a tight
  white foam on top.
</Step>
