<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to an extra-fine, almost powdery texture.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the grounds with the <Qty ref="water"/> and <Qty ref="sugar"/> in
  the cezve and stir to wet the grounds evenly.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Set the pot over low heat. As the surface begins to foam, lift it off the
  heat just before it boils over, then return it, repeating over
  <Dur step="cook"/> until the foam is thick and stable.
</Step>

<Step id="settle">
  Spoon the foam into the cup first, then pour the rest over it and let the
  grounds settle for <Dur step="settle"/> before drinking.
</Step>
