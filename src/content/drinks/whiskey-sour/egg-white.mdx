  - kind: technique
    stepRef: dry-shake
    text: >-
      The first shake has no ice in it on purpose. Protein unfolds and traps air
      far more readily warm than cold, so a shake without ice builds the foam and
      the shake with ice then chills it without knocking it back down. Doing it
      the other way round gives a thin, short-lived head.
  - kind: pitfall

      A white that has been sitting in the lemon juice will not whip. Acid starts
      denaturing the protein straight away, so the white goes in with everything
      else and the shaker closes immediately.

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="bourbon"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="white"/> in a shaker. Close it straight
  away; the white should not sit in the acid.
</Step>

<Step id="dry-shake">
  Shake with no ice at all for <Dur step="dry-shake"/>, until the shaker feels
  heavier than it should and the sound goes soft.
</Step>

<Step id="wet-shake">
  Open it, fill it with cubed ice and shake again for <Dur step="wet-shake"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a double old fashioned glass over fresh cubed ice, pouring
  slowly so the foam settles on top rather than folding into the drink.
</Step>
