<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp. Juice that has
  been sitting since yesterday makes a duller drink than anything else you could
  get wrong here.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="bourbon"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill and
  aerate, not to melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a double old fashioned glass over fresh cubed ice. The ice in the
  shaker has done its work and is half water by now.
</Step>
