<Step id="boil">
  Combine the <Qty ref="water"/> and the <Qty ref="tea"/> in a pan with the
  <Qty ref="soda"/> and simmer for <Dur step="boil"/>, until reduced and
  deep burgundy.
</Step>

<Step id="shock">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="ice-water"/> to deepen the colour further.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a clean pan, leaving the leaf behind.
</Step>

<Step id="whip">
  Bring the <Qty ref="milk"/> to a near boil, then pour it repeatedly back
  and forth between the pan and a jug from a height for <Dur step="whip"/>,
  until the tea turns pink and takes on a light froth. Stir in the
  <Qty ref="salt"/> and serve.
</Step>
