<Step id="start-bug">
  Stir the first portion of the <Qty ref="bug-feed"/> and
  <Qty ref="bug-sugar-feed"/> into the <Qty ref="bug-water"/> in a clean
  jar, cover loosely, and leave at room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="feed-bug">
  Feed the bug daily with a spoonful more grated ginger and sugar from the
  same portions until it reliably fizzes within a few hours of feeding.
</Step>

<Step id="brew">
  Stir the <Qty ref="beer-ginger"/> and the <Qty ref="beer-sugar"/> into
  the <Qty ref="main-water"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, then strain in
  the whole active bug.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  until it tastes sharp and faintly fizzy.
</Step>

<Step id="bottle">
  Strain into bottles rated for carbonation, leaving headspace, and cap
  tightly.
</Step>

<Step id="condition">
  Leave the sealed bottles at room temperature for <Dur step="condition"/>,
  burping each once a day, then refrigerate until cold before serving.
</Step>
