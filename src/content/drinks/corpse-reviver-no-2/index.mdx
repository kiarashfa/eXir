<Step id="rinse">
  Chill a coupe. Pour the <Qty ref="rinse"/> into it, tilt and swirl so the
  spirit coats the inside completely, then tip it out over the sink.
</Step>

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="liqueur"/>, the
  <Qty ref="lillet"/> and the <Qty ref="lemon"/> in a shaker and fill it with
  cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill
  and aerate, not to melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into the rinsed coupe, which holds no ice of its own.
</Step>
