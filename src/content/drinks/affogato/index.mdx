<Step id="scoop">
  Scoop the <Qty ref="gelato"/> into a chilled glass and return it to the
  freezer while the espresso is pulled.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> fine, close to table-salt texture, for a short
  shot rather than a filter brew.
</Step>

<Step id="extract">
  Pump the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={93}/> for
  <Dur step="extract"/>, watching for a thin, hazelnut-coloured crema on top.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the hot espresso directly over the gelato and serve at once, before the
  crema has time to break.
</Step>
