<Step id="sift">
  Sift the <Qty ref="matcha"/> into a bowl to break up any clumps.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk">
  Add the <Qty ref="water"/> just off the boil and whisk briskly in a
  zigzag for <Dur step="whisk"/>, until smooth and lightly foamed.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Steam the <Qty ref="milk"/> to a light microfoam.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the steamed milk into the glass over the whisked matcha, finishing
  with a thin cap of foam.
</Step>
