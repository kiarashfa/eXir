<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the lemons for the <Qty ref="lemon"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the pulp and seeds through a fine sieve.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the strained juice with the <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a glass
  filled with ice and stir once to combine.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Set the <Qty ref="garnish"/> lemon wheel on the rim.
</Step>
