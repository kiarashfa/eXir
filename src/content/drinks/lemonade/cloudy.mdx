<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the lemons for the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, leaving the pulp in rather than straining it
  out.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Stir the juice with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and the <Qty ref="water"/> in a glass filled with
  ice until the sugar dissolves.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Set the <Qty ref="garnish"/> lemon wheel on the rim.
</Step>
