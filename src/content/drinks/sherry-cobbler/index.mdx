<Step id="muddle">
  Lightly muddle two or three slices of the <Qty ref="garnish-orange"/> with
  the <Qty ref="syrup"/> in the base of the glass.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="sherry"/> and fill the glass with crushed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="churn">
  Churn with a bar spoon for <Dur step="churn"/> to bring the sherry up
  through the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Top with more crushed ice and spear the <Qty ref="garnish-berries"/> on a
  pick across the rim, with a straw.
</Step>
