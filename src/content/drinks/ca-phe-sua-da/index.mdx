<Step id="build">
  Spoon the <Qty ref="milk"/> into the bottom of the serving glass and set the
  phin filter on top.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to a medium-coarse texture and settle it into
  the phin's basket, then tighten the press plate just enough to sit flat.
</Step>

<Step id="drip">
  Pour the <Qty ref="water"/> at <Temp c={95}/> into the phin in two additions
  and let it drip through onto the condensed milk for <Dur step="drip"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Lift off the phin, stir the coffee and condensed milk together, then pour
  over a full glass of ice.
</Step>
