<Step id="steep">
  Pour the <Qty ref="water"/> at <Temp c={100}/> over the <Qty ref="tea"/>
  and steep for <Dur step="steep"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the leaf into a metal cup or tin.
</Step>

<Step id="mix">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="milk"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pull">
  Pour the tea back and forth between the cup and a second vessel held at
  arm's length, from a height, until a layer of froth sits on top.
</Step>
