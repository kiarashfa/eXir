<Step id="syrup">
  Simmer the <Qty ref="palm-sugar"/> with the <Qty ref="water"/> for
  <Dur step="syrup"/>, until it forms a thin syrup, then cool.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Fill a glass with crushed ice, spoon in the <Qty ref="jelly"/>, and pour
  the cooled palm sugar syrup over it.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the <Qty ref="coconut-milk"/> over the back of a spoon on top, and
  serve with a long spoon to stir it all together.
</Step>
