  Chill a coupe.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rye"/>, the <Qty ref="sweet-vermouth"/>, the

</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted, and

</Step>
