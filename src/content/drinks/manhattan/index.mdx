  Chill a coupe.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rye"/>, the <Qty ref="vermouth"/> and the
  <Qty ref="bitters"/> in a mixing glass filled with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted, and
  strain into the chilled coupe.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Drop the <Qty ref="cherry"/> into the glass to finish.
