  - kind: technique

    text: >-
      The banana is peeled, sliced and frozen solid before it goes anywhere
      near the jug. It is the drink's whole frozen solid slot, doing the work
      ice does in a frozen cocktail, and a fresh banana blended with ice cubes
      instead comes back thinner and waterier for it.
  - kind: pitfall

      built the vortex the milk is there for. Adding the milk first and the
      fruit after gives the blade something to move through from the start.

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="milk"/> to the blender jug first, then the frozen
  <Qty ref="banana"/> and the <Qty ref="honey"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend for <Dur step="blend"/>, until the banana has broken down completely
  and the mixture is uniform, thick and pourable rather than churning as
  loose chunks.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour into a chilled smoothie glass.
</Step>
