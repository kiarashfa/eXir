<Step id="dissolve">
  Stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the <Qty ref="water"/> until fully
  dissolved, then let it cool completely to room temperature.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Add the <Qty ref="grains"/> and the <Qty ref="raisins"/> to the cooled
  sugar water in a clean jar.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  away from direct light, until the liquid tastes tangy rather than
  sweet.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the grains and raisins, reserving the grains for the next
  batch, and refrigerate the kefir before serving.
</Step>
