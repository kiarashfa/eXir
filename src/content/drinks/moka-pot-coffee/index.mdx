<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to a medium-fine texture, finer than a drip
  grind but coarser than an espresso grind.
</Step>

<Step id="fill">
  Fill the base with the <Qty ref="water"/> below the pressure valve, seat
  the funnel and pack the grounds level without tamping, then screw the top
  chamber on.
</Step>

<Step id="brew">
  Set the pot over moderate heat with the lid open and listen for the flow.
  Brew for <Dur step="brew"/>, until the stream turns pale and starts to
  sputter.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Take the pot off the heat as soon as it sputters and pour at once.
</Step>
