  - kind: technique
    stepRef: top
    text: >-
      The soda is poured slowly down the side of the glass rather than straight onto the
      scoop, so it foams up around the ice cream into a head rather than fizzing over the rim.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Built too far ahead, the scoop melts into the soda before it is served and the drink
      loses the contrast between cold, solid cream and fizzing liquid that is the entire point
      of a float. It is assembled at the last moment, not held.
