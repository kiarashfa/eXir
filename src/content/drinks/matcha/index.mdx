<Step id="sift">
  Sift the <Qty ref="matcha"/> into the bowl to break up any clumps.
</Step>

<Step id="whisk">
  Add the <Qty ref="water"/> just off the boil and whisk briskly in a
  zigzag until the surface is covered in a fine, even foam.
</Step>
