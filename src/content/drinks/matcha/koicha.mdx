<Step id="sift">
  Sift the <Qty ref="matcha"/> into the bowl to break up any clumps.
</Step>

<Step id="knead">
  Add the <Qty ref="water"/> just off the boil in two additions, folding
  and kneading with the chasen after each until the paste is smooth,
  glossy and free of bubbles.
</Step>
