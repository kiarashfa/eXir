<Step id="soak">
  Soak the <Qty ref="tiger-nuts"/> in the <Qty ref="soak-water"/> for
  <Dur step="soak"/>, until fully softened.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend the soaked tiger nuts with their soaking water and the
  <Qty ref="blend-water"/> until as smooth as the blender will take it.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain through a cloth-lined strainer, pressing hard to get all the
  liquid through and leave the fibrous pulp behind, then stir in the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Refrigerate until fully cold before serving.
</Step>
