notes:
  - kind: technique
    stepRef: float
    text: >-

      stirred in, which is what leaves the pale layer sitting distinctly on
      top rather than clouding the whole glass into one even colour.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Cream stirred through rather than floated is not simply a different
      look. It also dilutes the top of the drink faster than the vodka and
      liqueur below it warm, so the first sip and the last taste noticeably
      unlike each other.

<Step id="build">
  Fill the glass with ice and pour in the <Qty ref="vodka"/> and
  <Qty ref="liqueur"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="float">
  Pour the <Qty ref="cream"/> over the back of a spoon held just above the
  surface, letting it settle as a pale layer on top.
</Step>
