<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to a medium texture for a drip brew.
</Step>

<Step id="brew">
  Brew the <Qty ref="coffee"/> with the <Qty ref="water"/> at <Temp c={94}/>
  over <Dur step="brew"/> to give a strong, dark cup.
</Step>

<Step id="scald">
  While the coffee brews, heat the <Qty ref="milk"/> in a small pan just to
  the point of steaming, without letting it boil.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the hot coffee and the scalded milk into a wide bowl or mug together,
  in two streams at once.
</Step>
