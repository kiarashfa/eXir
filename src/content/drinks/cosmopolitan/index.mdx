<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="vodka"/>, the <Qty ref="liqueur"/>, the
  <Qty ref="lime"/> and the <Qty ref="cranberry"/> in a shaker and fill it
  with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> until the tin frosts over.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled martini glass, which holds no ice of its own.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a wide strip of orange peel, express it skin-outwards over the surface
  so the oil lands on the drink, then either drop it in or set it across the
  rim.
</Step>
