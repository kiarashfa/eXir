<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> coarse, close to the texture of sea salt.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Add the grounds to the pot and pour the <Qty ref="water"/> over them at
  <Temp c={94}/>, wetting all of the grounds, then set the lid on without
  plunging.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Leave to steep for <Dur step="steep"/>, undisturbed.
</Step>

<Step id="plunge">
  Press the plunger down slowly and evenly over <Dur step="plunge"/>, then
  pour at once.
</Step>
