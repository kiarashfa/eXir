<Step id="rim">
  Run a lime wedge around half the rim of a poco grande glass and dip that
  half in salt.
</Step>

<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Combine the <Qty ref="tequila"/>, the <Qty ref="liqueur"/>, the
  <Qty ref="lime"/> and the <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a blender jug, add the
  <Qty ref="ice"/> and blend in short bursts for <Dur step="blend"/>, until it
  forms an even slush with no loose chunks left clicking against the blade.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour, unstrained, into the rimmed glass and serve with a straw before it
  starts to separate.
</Step>
