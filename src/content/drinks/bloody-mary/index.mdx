<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="vodka"/>, the <Qty ref="tomato"/>, the
  <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the <Qty ref="worcestershire"/>, the
  <Qty ref="hotsauce"/>, the <Qty ref="celerysalt"/> and the <Qty ref="pepper"/>
  in a mixing tin with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="throw">
  Throw the drink: pour it into a second tin held low, then back into the
  first, repeating for <Dur step="throw"/>. The stream of liquid does the
  mixing, not the ice knocking against the tin.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Strain into a highball filled with fresh cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a wedge from the <Qty ref="garnish-lemon"/> and set it on the rim.
</Step>
