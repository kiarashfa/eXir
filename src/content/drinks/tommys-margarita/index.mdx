<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="tequila"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/> and the
  <Qty ref="agave"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake for <Dur step="shake"/> until the tin frosts over.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain over fresh cubed ice into a rocks glass and drop in the lime
  wheel.
</Step>
