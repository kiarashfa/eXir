<Step id="soak">
  Soak the <Qty ref="soybeans"/> in the <Qty ref="soak-water"/> for
  <Dur step="soak"/>, until fully softened and swollen.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Drain the beans and blend them with the <Qty ref="grind-water"/> until
  smooth.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain the mixture through muslin, pressing hard to get all the milk
  through and leave the spent pulp behind.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Bring the strained milk to a full boil, watching closely as it rises,
  then lower the heat and simmer for <Dur step="boil"/>, stirring the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> through before serving warm.
</Step>
