<Step id="build">
  Hull and roughly chop the <Qty ref="strawberry"/> and blend briefly with
  the <Qty ref="milk"/>, then add the <Qty ref="ice-cream"/> straight from
  the freezer.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend for <Dur step="blend"/>, until the ice cream and berries have broken
  down into an even, thick shake with no unmelted lumps or fruit skin left.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour into a chilled milkshake glass.
</Step>
