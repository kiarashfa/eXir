notes:
  - kind: technique
    stepRef: build
    text: >-
      The ice cream is the frozen solid slot here, not an addition to a
      liquid base: the milk exists to give the blade a vortex to work with,
      and a milkshake carries far less of it than a smoothie does because
      the ice cream itself is already most of the volume.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Ice cream left to soften before it goes in blends smoother but comes
      back thin, because it has already lost the air and structure that
      made it a solid rather than a flavoured milk.
  - kind: technique
    stepRef: build
    text: >-
      The extract is held to half a teaspoon for a reason the label does not
      explain: vanilla extract is a spirit, bottled at about the strength of
      a light rum, and a full teaspoon in a shake this size takes the finished
      drink past the strength at which it stops being non-alcoholic. It reads
      as flavouring and it is measured like flavouring, but it is the only
      alcohol in the glass.

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="milk"/> to the blender jug first, then the
  <Qty ref="ice-cream"/> straight from the freezer and the <Qty ref="vanilla"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend for <Dur step="blend"/>, just long enough for the ice cream to break
  down into an even, thick shake with no unmelted lumps left.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour into a chilled milkshake glass.
</Step>
