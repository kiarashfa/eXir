<Step id="build">
  Blend the <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="cocoa"/> briefly to dissolve
  the powder, then add the <Qty ref="ice-cream"/> straight from the freezer.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend for <Dur step="blend"/>, until the ice cream has broken down into an
  even, thick shake with no unmelted lumps or dry cocoa left.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour into a chilled milkshake glass.
</Step>
