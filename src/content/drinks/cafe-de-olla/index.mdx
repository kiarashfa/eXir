<Step id="dissolve">
  Simmer the <Qty ref="water"/> with the <Qty ref="piloncillo"/> and
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> in a clay or heavy pot for <Dur step="dissolve"/>,
  until the piloncillo is fully dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> coarse, close to sea-salt texture.
</Step>

<Step id="steep">
  Stir in the ground coffee, take the pot off the heat and let it steep for
  <Dur step="steep"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into the serving mug, leaving the grounds and the spent cinnamon
  behind in the pot.
</Step>
