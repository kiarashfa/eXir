<Step id="whisk">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="egg-white"/>, the <Qty ref="egg-yolk"/> and the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> together until the sugar has dissolved.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Whisk in the <Qty ref="bourbon"/>, the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the
  <Qty ref="milk"/> and the <Qty ref="cream"/> until fully combined.
</Step>

<Step id="age">
  Seal and refrigerate for three weeks before tasting. The mixture
  thickens and mellows over the rest; do not open and stir it in the
  meantime.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Pour into mugs and grate the <Qty ref="garnish-nutmeg"/> over the top.
</Step>
