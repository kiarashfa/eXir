<Step id="whisk">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="egg-white"/> and <Qty ref="egg-yolk"/> with the
  <Qty ref="sugar"/> together until pale.
</Step>

<Step id="temper">
  Warm the <Qty ref="milk"/> until it steams, then pour it into the egg
  mixture in a thin stream while whisking, so the eggs come up to
  temperature gradually rather than scrambling on contact.
</Step>

<Step id="cook">
  Return everything to the pan and cook over low heat, stirring constantly,
  until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="bourbon"/>, the <Qty ref="cream"/> and the
  <Qty ref="vanilla"/>, then cover and refrigerate until fully cold.
</Step>

<Step id="finish">
  Pour into mugs and grate the <Qty ref="garnish-nutmeg"/> over the top.
</Step>
