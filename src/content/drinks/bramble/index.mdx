<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="ice">
  Pack a double rocks glass with crushed ice, mounded above the rim so it
  will still be visible once the liquid is poured over it.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Pour the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the <Qty ref="syrup"/> directly over the
  crushed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir briefly for <Dur step="stir"/>, just enough to settle the ice and combine the three liquids
  — this is a build, not a stir to equilibrium, so the drink is still diluting after it is served.
</Step>

<Step id="drizzle">
  Pour the <Qty ref="mure"/> over the surface in a slow, circular motion so it sits as a layer and
  bleeds downward through the ice rather than mixing straight in.
</Step>
