<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> fine for the espresso machine.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Steam the <Qty ref="milk"/> to a thick, stiff foam over <Dur step="steam"/>,
  well beyond the light microfoam a latte needs.
</Step>

<Step id="extract">
  Pump the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={93}/> for
  <Dur step="extract"/> while the milk steams.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the steamed milk into the cup over the shot, holding back the foam
  with a spoon until last so it sits as a full collar rather than sinking in.
</Step>
