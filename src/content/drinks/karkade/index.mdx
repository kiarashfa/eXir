<Step id="boil">
  Bring the <Qty ref="water"/> to a boil, add the <Qty ref="hibiscus"/> and
  simmer for <Dur step="boil"/>, until the liquid is a deep ruby red.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a cup, leaving the spent calyces behind.
</Step>

<Step id="sweeten">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> while the tea is still hot enough to
  dissolve it.
</Step>
