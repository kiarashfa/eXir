<Step id="roast">
  Roast the green beans in a flat pan over low heat, shaking constantly, until
  they darken and turn glossy with their own oil.
</Step>

<Step id="grind">
  Grind the roasted beans to a fine powder with a mortar and pestle to make
  the <Qty ref="coffee"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Add the <Qty ref="water"/> to the jebena with the grounds and bring it to a
  boil over the fire three times, letting it settle back each time, for a
  total of <Dur step="boil"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="settle">
  Stand the jebena upright for <Dur step="settle"/> so the grounds sink to the
  bottom, then pour from a height into the small cups without disturbing them.
</Step>
