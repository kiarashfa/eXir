<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="pineapple"/> and the
  <Qty ref="cream"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> to fully emulsify the cream of coconut
  through the juice and rum.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled hurricane glass, catching the ice shards the
  shake has broken off.
</Step>
