<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="pineapple"/> and the
  <Qty ref="cream"/> in a blender jug, then add the <Qty ref="ice"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="blend">
  Blend in short bursts for <Dur step="blend"/>, until the mixture climbs the
  sides of the jug as an even, pourable slush with no loose chunks left
  clicking against the blade.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour, unstrained, into a chilled hurricane glass and serve with a straw
  before it starts to separate.
</Step>
