<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="pineapple"/>, the <Qty ref="piloncillo"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/> and the <Qty ref="cloves"/> in a large jar and pour
  over the <Qty ref="water"/>, stirring until the piloncillo dissolves.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  away from direct light, skimming any foam that rises.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain out the solids and refrigerate until cold before serving.
</Step>
