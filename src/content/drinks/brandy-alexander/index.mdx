<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="cognac"/>, the <Qty ref="cacao"/> and the <Qty ref="cream"/> in a
  shaker with ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>, longer than a citrus sour needs, so the cream takes on a
  full head of air.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a chilled coupe.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Grate the <Qty ref="garnish-nutmeg"/> over the surface.
</Step>
