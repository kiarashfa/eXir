<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lime"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="lime"/>, the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="bitters"/> in a shaker and fill it
  with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain over fresh cubed ice into a chilled Collins glass.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a swathe of orange peel, express it over the surface and drop it in,
  then set the <Qty ref="mint"/> beside the straw.
</Step>
