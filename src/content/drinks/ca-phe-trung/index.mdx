<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to a medium-fine texture for the phin.
</Step>

<Step id="drip">
  Set the phin over the serving cup and let the <Qty ref="water"/> at
  <Temp c={95}/> drip through for <Dur step="drip"/>, leaving hot black coffee
  in the bottom of the cup.
</Step>

<Step id="whip">
  In a separate bowl, whisk the <Qty ref="yolk"/> and <Qty ref="milk"/>
  together for <Dur step="whip"/>, until pale, thick and roughly doubled in
  volume.
</Step>

<Step id="float">
  Spoon the whipped yolk over the hot coffee so it sits as a distinct layer on
  top, and serve at once with a spoon.
</Step>
