<Step id="shake">
  Combine the <Qty ref="coffee"/>, <Qty ref="sugar"/> and
  <Qty ref="shake-water"/> in a shaker and shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>,
  until it turns to a thick, pale foam.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Fill the glass with ice, pour the <Qty ref="top-water"/> over it, then
  spoon the foam on top.
</Step>
