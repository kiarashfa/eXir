<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to a medium-fine texture, a little coarser than
  table salt.
</Step>

<Step id="rinse">
  Set the paper filter in the cone and rinse it through with hot water, then
  discard what collects underneath. This washes the paper and warms the vessel;
  an unrinsed filter tastes of itself.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom">
  Pour the <Qty ref="bloom-water"/> at <Temp c={94}/> over the grounds, wetting
  all of them, and leave it for <Dur step="bloom"/>. The bed will swell and
  foam.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Add the <Qty ref="brew-water"/> over <Dur step="pour"/>, pouring in a slow
  spiral and keeping the bed level. The drawdown should finish shortly after the
  last of the water goes in.
</Step>
