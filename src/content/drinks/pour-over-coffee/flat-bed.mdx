<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> a shade coarser than you would for a cone. A flat
  bed drains more slowly at the same grind, and the extra contact is already in
  the recipe.
</Step>

<Step id="rinse">
  Set the wave filter in the dripper, open its pleats and rinse it through with
  hot water. Discard what collects underneath.
</Step>

<Step id="bloom">
  Pour the <Qty ref="bloom-water"/> at <Temp c={93}/> over the grounds and leave
  it for <Dur step="bloom"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Add the <Qty ref="brew-water"/> over <Dur step="pour"/> in three even
  additions, pouring into the middle and letting the level fall between them.
</Step>
