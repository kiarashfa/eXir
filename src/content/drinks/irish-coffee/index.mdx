<Step id="grind">
  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> to a medium texture for a drip brew.
</Step>

<Step id="brew">
  Brew the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={95}/> for
  <Dur step="brew"/>, straight into the warmed serving glass.
</Step>

<Step id="sweeten">
  Stir the <Qty ref="sugar"/> into the hot coffee until fully dissolved, then
  stir in the <Qty ref="whiskey"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="whip">
  Whip the <Qty ref="cream"/> for <Dur step="whip"/>, just enough to thicken
  it to a pourable, self-supporting consistency.
</Step>

<Step id="float">
  Pour the cream over the back of a spoon held just above the surface, so it
  spreads out and sits as a distinct layer on top.
</Step>
