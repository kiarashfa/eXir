<Step id="chill">
  Chill a rocks glass and set a single large cube in it. One large piece melts
  more slowly than the same mass broken into small ones, and the drink is meant
  to be drunk slowly.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="campari"/> and the
  <Qty ref="vermouth"/> in a mixing glass filled with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted and the
  liquid has visibly gained volume. Strain over the waiting cube.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Cut a <Len cm={4}/> swathe of orange peel, hold it skin-outwards over the
  surface and squeeze so the oil lands on the drink. Rub it around the rim and
  drop it in.
</Step>
