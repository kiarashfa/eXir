<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> in a shaker and fill it with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/> to chill and aerate the drink before any
  wine reaches it.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain up into a chilled flute, with no ice in the glass itself.
</Step>

<Step id="top">
  Top with the <Qty ref="sparkling"/>, poured gently down the side of the
  glass so the foam it raises has somewhere to go.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Twist a strip of lemon peel over the surface to release its oil, then drop
  it in.
</Step>
