<Step id="chill">
  Chill a martini glass.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, the <Qty ref="dry-vermouth"/> and the
  <Qty ref="sweet-vermouth"/> in a mixing glass filled with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, until the outside of the glass is frosted, and
  strain into the chilled glass.
</Step>

<Step id="garnish">
  Skewer the <Qty ref="olive"/> on a pick and drop it in.
</Step>
