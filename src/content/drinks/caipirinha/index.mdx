<Step id="cut">
  Cut the <Qty ref="lime"/> into six to eight wedges.
</Step>

<Step id="muddle">
  Muddle the lime wedges with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> in the base of the glass
  for <Dur step="muddle"/>, pressing to release the juice and the oils from
  the peel without pulverising the pith.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="cachaca"/>, fill the glass with crushed ice and stir
  briefly to combine.
</Step>
