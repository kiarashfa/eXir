<Step id="boil">
  Combine the <Qty ref="water"/>, the <Qty ref="tea"/>, the
  <Qty ref="cardamom"/> and the <Qty ref="sugar"/> in a small pan, bring to a
  boil, then lower the heat and simmer for <Dur step="boil"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="addmilk">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="milk"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="reboil">
  Bring back to a boil and simmer again for <Dur step="reboil"/>, until the
  colour is an even, deep tan.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain into a cup, leaving the leaf and cardamom behind.
</Step>
