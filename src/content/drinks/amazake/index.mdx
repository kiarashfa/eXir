<Step id="combine">
  Stir the <Qty ref="koji"/> into the still-warm <Qty ref="rice"/> along with
  the <Qty ref="mash-water"/>, breaking up any clumps of koji as it goes in.
</Step>

<Step id="ferment">
  Hold the mash at <Temp c={55}/> to <Temp c={60}/> for <Dur step="ferment"/>,
  covered but not sealed, stirring it once or twice. It is ready when it
  tastes distinctly sweet and the grains have mostly broken down.
</Step>

<Step id="thin">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="serve-water"/>, warmed through, to loosen the mash to
  a drinkable consistency, and ladle it into cups while it is still warm.
</Step>
