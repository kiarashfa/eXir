<Step id="dissolve">
  Tear the <Qty ref="kasu"/> into pieces and whisk it into the <Qty ref="water"/>
  over medium heat until it has fully broken down into a smooth, cloudy liquid.
</Step>

<Step id="sweeten">
  Stir in the <Qty ref="sugar"/> and hold the pot at a bare simmer until it
  dissolves.
</Step>

<Step id="grate">
  Ladle into cups and finish each with a little of the <Qty ref="ginger"/>,
  grated straight over the top.
</Step>
