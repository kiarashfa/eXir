<Step id="juice">
  Squeeze the <Qty ref="lemon"/> and strain out the pips and pulp.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="scotch"/>, the <Qty ref="lemon"/>, the
  <Qty ref="honey"/> and the <Qty ref="ginger"/> in a shaker and fill it with
  cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Strain over a single large cube in a rocks glass.
</Step>

<Step id="float">
  Pour the <Qty ref="float"/> over the back of a spoon so it settles on the
  surface as its own layer.
</Step>
