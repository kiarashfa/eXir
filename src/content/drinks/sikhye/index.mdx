<Step id="steep-malt">
  Steep the <Qty ref="malt"/> in the <Qty ref="malt-water"/>, warmed
  through, then strain out the grit and keep the cloudy malt water.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Stir the <Qty ref="rice"/> and the <Qty ref="brew-water"/> into the malt
  water.
</Step>

<Step id="saccharify">
  Hold the mixture at <Temp c={60}/> to <Temp c={65}/> for
  <Dur step="saccharify"/>, until a good share of the rice grains have
  risen and are floating on the surface.
</Step>

<Step id="boil">
  Strain out the rice, setting the floating grains aside, and bring the
  liquid to a boil with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> stirred through.
</Step>

<Step id="chill">
  Cool completely and refrigerate until cold, then float the reserved rice
  and the <Qty ref="pine-nuts"/> on top of each glass just before serving.
</Step>
