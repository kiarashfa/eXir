notes:
  - kind: technique
    text: >-
      Angostura is measured here as the base spirit rather than a dash, which inverts the
      usual proportion between a bitters and everything else in the glass. The rye is a
      backbone rather than the point of the drink, present mostly to keep the bitters and
      orgeat from reading as a cordial.
  - kind: pitfall
    text: >-
      Poured to a standard bitters dash, this recipe looks like a typo. It is not — the
      quantity of Angostura is what makes the drink what it is, and halving it in the name of
      caution turns it into a thin, sweet lemon drink with no backbone.
