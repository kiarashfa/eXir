<Step id="muddle">
  Press the <Qty ref="basil"/> in the bottom of a shaker with the
  <Qty ref="lemon"/> and the <Qty ref="syrup"/>, just enough to bruise the
  leaves and release their oil.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Add the <Qty ref="gin"/> and fill the shaker with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard for <Dur step="shake"/>. Short and violent; the aim is to chill
  and aerate, not to melt the ice.
</Step>

<Step id="strain">
  Double strain into a double old fashioned glass over fresh cubed ice,
  catching the torn leaf the shake has broken off.
</Step>
