<Step id="slurry">
  Whisk the <Qty ref="salep"/> with a small spoonful of the cold
  <Qty ref="milk"/> to a smooth paste.
</Step>

<Step id="heat">
  Warm the rest of the milk with the <Qty ref="sugar"/> over
  <Dur step="heat"/>, whisking in the paste, until thickened to a texture
  that coats the back of a spoon.
</Step>

<Step id="dust">
  Pour into a warmed mug and dust the surface with finely grated
  <Qty ref="cinnamon"/>.
</Step>
