  Grind the <Qty ref="coffee"/> fine for the espresso machine.
</Step>

<Step id="steam">
  Steam the <Qty ref="milk"/> to a light, silky microfoam, without letting it
  scald.
</Step>

<Step id="extract">
  Pump the <Qty ref="water"/> through the grounds at <Temp c={93}/> for
  <Dur step="extract"/> while the milk steams.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Pour the steamed milk into the glass over the shot, finishing with a thin
  cap of foam on top.
</Step>
