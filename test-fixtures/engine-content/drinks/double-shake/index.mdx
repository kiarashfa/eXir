<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/> and the <Qty ref="lime"/> in a shaker.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake hard with cubed ice for <Dur step="shake"/>, then double strain into a
  chilled coupe.
</Step>

<Step id="pour">
  Float the second lot of mint on the surface, where the reader meets it before
  the first sip rather than after.
</Step>
