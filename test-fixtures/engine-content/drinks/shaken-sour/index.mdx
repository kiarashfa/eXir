<Step id="rim">
  Wipe the rim of a chilled coupe with the <Qty ref="lime-rim"/>.
</Step>

<Step id="build">
  Combine the <Qty ref="rum"/>, the <Qty ref="lime-shake"/>, the
  <Qty ref="syrup"/> and the <Qty ref="white"/> in a shaker.
</Step>

<Step id="shake">
  Shake without ice, then again with it, for <Dur step="shake"/> in total.
  Double strain into the coupe.
</Step>
