<Step id="chill">
  Chill a rocks glass and drop in a single large cube. One large piece melts
  slower than the same mass in small ones.
</Step>

<Step id="combine">
  Combine the <Qty ref="gin"/>, <Qty ref="campari"/> and <Qty ref="vermouth"/>
  in a mixing glass filled with cubed ice.
</Step>

<Step id="stir">
  Stir for <Dur step="stir"/>, then strain over the waiting cube and express an
  orange twist across the surface.
</Step>
