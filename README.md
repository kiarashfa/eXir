# eXir

A free, ad-free, login-free drinks encyclopedia. One page per drink.

Every number on a page — quantities, dilution, final volume, strength, standard
drinks, sugar, energy, brewing ratios and timing — is computed from structured
data rather than typed into prose. Change the drink count and the whole page
recomputes; switch to US customary and the measures follow, in the method text
as well as the checklist. Nothing is hand-typed, so nothing can drift out of
step.

The arithmetic is the point rather than a discipline. Dilution, batching,
strength and brew ratio are the calculations working bartenders do constantly
and home drinkers get wrong, so the site does them: how much water a stir adds,
what a batch of twelve needs in place of the ice it will never meet, and why the
same drink made both ways is the same drink.

Free, with no ads, no accounts and nothing to subscribe to. Anything the site
remembers about you, such as your shelf, your plan, your units and your theme,
lives in your own browser.
